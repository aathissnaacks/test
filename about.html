Our Story

Born from a Gap
in the Market

Walk into any supermarket today and you will find rows of factory-made snacks — uniform, preservative-laden, and completely devoid of the soul that makes food memorable. That is the problem Aathi's Snaacks was created to solve.

Founded in 2024 in Thoothukudi — a coastal city in Tamil Nadu famous for its Tuty Macroons and traditional sweets — Aathi's Snaacks began with a simple mission: make authentic, homemade-quality snacks accessible to everyone across India, not just those lucky enough to live near a traditional sweet shop.

Every product we make is crafted by hand, using recipes passed down through generations, with no artificial preservatives, no shortcuts, and no compromise on taste. We are not a factory — we are a snack house.

Meet the Founder

Alagu Panduva
Murthy S.

A native of Thoothukudi, Alagu Panduva Murthy S. grew up surrounded by the rich snack culture of coastal Tamil Nadu — the aroma of freshly roasted kadalai, the sweetness of karupatti mittai, and the iconic crunch of Tuty Macroons.

After years of watching the market flood with mass-produced, flavourless alternatives, he decided to do something about it. In 2024, he founded Aathi's Snaacks under the Aathi Shivaya Group of Companies — not just as a business, but as a mission to preserve and share authentic flavours.

"I was tired of seeing our traditional snacks being replaced by factory products that taste of nothing. I wanted anyone, anywhere in India, to be able to close their eyes, take a bite, and feel like they are back home."

— Alagu Panduva Murthy S.
