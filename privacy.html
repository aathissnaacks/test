4. Payment Information

All payments on Aathi's Snaacks are processed via UPI (Unified Payments Interface) — a payment system regulated by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and operated by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI).

How Our Payment Process Works

  • You pay directly through your own UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, or any UPI-enabled app) to our UPI ID: 9342953995@okbizaxis
  • You may optionally upload a screenshot of your payment as proof
  • We manually verify the payment on our end
  • Your order is confirmed and dispatched after verification

Important: We never receive, store, or process your UPI PIN, bank account number, card number, or any sensitive financial credentials. The payment happens entirely within your UPI app and is governed by your bank's and UPI app's privacy policies.

Payment screenshots you upload are stored securely and used only to verify your payment. They are not shared with any third party.

5. How We Share Your Information

5.1 Delivery Partners

We share your name, phone number, and delivery address with our courier/logistics partners (e.g., India Post, Speed Post, or third-party courier services) solely for the purpose of delivering your order.

5.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or government authority, including tax authorities (GSTIN compliance) and food safety authorities (FSSAI).

5.3 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or a portion of our assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. You will be notified via email or prominent notice on our website.

5.4 Firebase / Google Cloud

We use Google Firebase (a Google LLC product) to store order information securely in the cloud. Firebase is compliant with major data protection standards. Your order data is stored on Firebase servers located in the Asia South region (Mumbai, India). Google's Privacy Policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

7. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses minimal cookies and local storage for the following purposes:

  • Shopping cart: We store your cart contents in your browser's local storage so your selections are saved across page visits. This data never leaves your device.
  • Language preference: We store your language preference (English or Tamil) in local storage.
  • Session authentication: If you access the admin panel, your login session is stored locally in your browser.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or behavioural analytics tools. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar tracking services.

You can clear your browser's local storage at any time through your browser settings. This will reset your cart and language preferences.

  • Payment screenshots are stored securely and accessible only to our team

Note: No method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.
